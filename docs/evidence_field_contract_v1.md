# Evidence Field Contract v1

This contract operationalizes the existing Evidence Matrix v0.4 schema without changing its structure. Populate a field only from explicit abstract text and preserve a verbatim support span. A mention in the title, common scientific knowledge, or the topic profile is not evidence. `null`, `[]`, or `unknown` is preferable to an unsupported value.

The examples and counterexamples below are schematic unless explicitly identified as an abstract quotation. A single source phrase may support more than one scientific fact, but it should not be copied into multiple categories unless each field's distinct definition is met.

## Study system

| Field | Definition | Include | Exclude | Boundary rule | Review-paper rule | Example | Counterexample |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `study_system.crop` | Crop or plant taxon that is an object of the present study. | Explicitly studied, sampled, grown, or analyzed crops. | Crops in examples, comparisons, feedstock, or literature summaries. | A crop used to produce an amendment is not automatically the study crop; list only plants investigated. | Record crops in the review scope only when the review explicitly names them as scope; do not imply the review authors grew them. | Schematic: `rice` grown in a stated treatment study. | `rice straw biochar` when only the amendment is studied. |
| `study_system.soil_type` | Explicit soil or substrate classification for the studied system. | Soil names and soil classes explicitly tied to the site, sample, or experiment. | Salinity severity alone; a region's general soils; soil mentioned only as a mechanism/application background. | Keep material identity here; place a study system's salinity condition in `salinity_context`. A phrase may populate both only when the abstract states both facts. | State a review's soil scope as reviewed context, not as a single sampled system. | Abstract: `coastal soil` (WOS:001631731300001). | `salinity of 2.60 g kg−1` alone is a salinity measurement, not a soil-type name. |
| `study_system.salinity_context` | Salinity state, level, or explicit saline setting of the actual system being studied. | Article: direct link to the experimental system, sampled site, or selected study area. Review: a clearly named review scope, attributed as scope. | Generic motivation, mechanism/application discussion, an unlinked regional statement, or salt stress appearing elsewhere in the abstract. | Require a source phrase that links the salinity descriptor to the actual study system/area. Separate actual-system context from generic stress discussion and treatment-induced salinity. | A Review may record `review scope: saline-alkali soils` when explicit; never present it as the review authors' own experiment or sampled system. | Abstract: `saline-affected paddy fields` monitored in the field (WOS:001602265100001). | `release kinetics in saline- and alkali-stressed environments` in a hydrogel review is generic mechanism context, not an empirical study system. |
| `study_system.location` | Named geographic place of the actual study or sampling system. | Explicit site, region, province, country, or coordinates tied to the study. | A place in background, affiliation, comparison, or unrelated prior work. | Do not infer place from journal, author affiliation, crop, or topic. | Record geography as review coverage only when explicitly stated; do not imply one empirical site. | Abstract: `western Jilin Province (WJP)` selected as the study region (WOS:001487641900001). | `Northeast China` as a general distribution statement when it is not tied to the selected system. |
| `study_system.experimental_scale` | One schema enum describing the explicitly stated setting or scale. | Only direct phrases mapped by this rule: `field`, `field-based`, `field study` → `field`; `pot experiment`, `pot study` → `pot`; `greenhouse` → `greenhouse`; `laboratory`, `incubation` → `lab`; `model`, `modelling`, `modeling` → `model`. | A scale inferred from an instrument, crop, method name, location, or research topic. | Exact enum only: `field`, `pot`, `greenhouse`, `lab`, `model`, or `unknown`. A listed scale phrase must refer to this paper's study design; do not fuzzy-match enum values. | Keep `unknown` unless the Review abstract explicitly identifies its own analysis scale; scales of cited studies are not the Review authors' scale. | Abstract: `a pot experiment was conducted` (WOS:001713822800001) → `pot`. | `HYDRUS-3D model simulated...` alone names a method/model, not a field or pot experiment. |

## Treatments

| Field | Definition | Include | Exclude | Boundary rule | Review-paper rule | Example | Counterexample |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `treatments.irrigation` | Water-supply or irrigation application used as an actual study treatment or stated irrigation method. | Explicit irrigation source, application amount/timing, or named irrigation treatment. | Observed water availability, irrigation demand, or a scenario not applied as a treatment. | Use for the application/method of supplying water; use `water_regime` for the resulting sustained water-state pattern. Do not duplicate the same phrase in both. | Reviewed irrigation interventions may be recorded only as review-synthesis evidence with attribution, never as the Review authors' treatment. | Schematic: `irrigation at 20 mm per event` as an applied treatment. | `irrigation availability controlled paddy expansion` is an observational driver. |
| `treatments.water_regime` | Explicit sustained soil/crop water-state regime or cycle imposed or compared. | Flooded, AWD, alternate wetting and drying, continuous flooding, or wet-dry cycles when used as the study regime. | Water delivery amount/source, an irrigation system name without state, or observed rainfall/groundwater. | A phrase such as `flooded and shallow-wet` belongs here when it names water-state regimes; do not copy it to irrigation merely because the paper calls them irrigation modes. | Regimes discussed from prior literature retain review attribution and are not author-operated regimes. | Abstract: `flooded and shallow–wet` conditions compared in a pot study (WOS:001713822800001). | `irrigation water volume` is a water-use/application quantity, not a regime. |
| `treatments.amendments` | Material deliberately added, incorporated, or applied to alter soil or substrate. | Explicit organic/mineral materials, biochar, straw, gypsum, or formulated soil amendments. | Live organisms, fertilizer nutrient rates unless described as soil amendment, and naturally present soil properties. | A bio-derived solid material remains an amendment; a live microbe/alga belongs in `biological_treatments`. Preserve type and dose if stated. | A Review may synthesize amendment classes, but must not describe them as applied by its authors. | Abstract: rice/corn/cow-manure biochar treatments (WOS:001631731300001). | Chlorella as a live biological agent is not a material amendment. |
| `treatments.fertilization` | Nutrient fertilizer product, formulation, rate, timing, or placement used as a treatment. | Explicit fertilizer inputs and fertilizer-management treatments. | Nitrogen concentration measured in soil/water/plant; nitrogen uptake result; non-fertilizer soil amendments. | Separate the applied nutrient input from measured nutrient pools/fluxes in `measurements.nitrogen`. | Fertilizer strategies from reviewed studies are synthesis content, not the Review authors' treatments. | Abstract: polyurethane-coated urea/urea mixture treatments (WOS:001859510400001). | `nitrogen uptake increased` is a measurement/result, not a fertilizer treatment. |
| `treatments.biological_treatments` | Live organism, inoculum, biological agent, or biologically active treatment deliberately applied. | Explicit inoculation, microbial/algal application, seed/plant biological treatment, or organism-based intervention. | Microbial community measured as an outcome; organism merely observed or mentioned. | Distinguish biological treatment from bio-derived material: live Chlorella is biological; biochar is an amendment. | Reviewed biological interventions are attributed to the studies reviewed. | Abstract: Chlorella application in the wastewater-reuse experiment (WOS:001713822800001). | `microbial diversity increased` is a microbial measurement/result, not a biological treatment. |
| `treatments.other_treatments` | Explicit applied experimental factor that does not fit the named treatment fields. | Other controlled intervention or treatment arm stated as applied/compared. | Scenario labels in modeling, climate variables, observed drivers, controls as mere design metadata, and findings. | Require a direct application/manipulation relation. Keep modeled scenarios and normal/dry years out unless the study actually imposes them. | Do not attribute reviewed treatments to Review authors. | Abstract: straw removal/incorporation/burial treatment arms (WOS:001859510400001). | `eight different scenarios were designed` for a regional model is not an applied treatment. |

## Measurements

| Field | Definition | Include | Exclude | Boundary rule | Review-paper rule | Example | Counterexample |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `measurements.soil_physical` | Measured soil physical structure or physical property. | Bulk density, porosity, aggregate stability, texture, infiltration, or explicitly measured physical soil properties. | Chemical concentrations, microbial outcomes, crop growth, or treatment materials. | Classify by the measured property, not by the instrument or method. | Review synthesis of physical properties is not a measurement performed by the Review authors. | Schematic: measured soil bulk density. | `biochar was applied` is a treatment. |
| `measurements.soil_chemical` | Measured chemical property of soil other than the separately tracked nitrogen/carbon/gas categories. | Soil pH, electrical conductivity, salinity, ions, or chemical composition when explicitly measured. | Nitrogen pools/fluxes, carbon pools, treatment dose, or a generic soil name. | Use the specific `nitrogen`, `carbon`, or `greenhouse_gases` field when the measured entity is one of those; keep soil salinity measurements here, while system salinity context belongs in `study_system.salinity_context`. | Attribute review-reported chemical outcomes to the reviewed literature. | Abstract: seasonal soil salinity changes in farmland (WOS:001602265100001). | `saline-alkali soil` without a measurement is soil/context description, not a chemical measurement. |
| `measurements.carbon` | Measured carbon pool, fraction, content, stock, or carbon flux not already classified as a greenhouse gas. | Soil organic carbon, total organic carbon, carbon stock, or explicit carbon sequestration measurement. | Biochar material presence, carbon-based treatment identity, or CO₂/CH₄/N₂O flux. | Carbon analytes/pools go here; gaseous emissions go to `greenhouse_gases`. | Review-reported carbon outcomes remain synthesis, not author measurements. | Schematic: measured soil organic carbon stock. | `biochar amendment` alone is not a carbon measurement. |
| `measurements.nitrogen` | Measured nitrogen pool, concentration, uptake, transport, removal, or nitrogen-use metric. | Nitrate/ammonium, total N, N uptake, N transport/removal, or NUE when explicitly measured/reported. | Fertilizer application/rate, phosphorus-only measures, or generic nutrient treatment. | Use the analyte or flux field, not `soil_chemical` by default; nitrogen input belongs in `fertilization`. | Review synthesis of nitrogen outcomes is not a Review-author measurement. | Abstract: nitrogen transport/removal in water (WOS:001713822800001). | Urea application rate is fertilization, not measured nitrogen. |
| `measurements.microbial` | Measured microorganisms, microbial communities, diversity, abundance, or microbial activity. | Community composition/structure, microbial diversity, microbial biomass, or named microbial indices. | An applied live organism, a plant outcome, or an unnamed general soil property. | Measurement of microbial properties belongs here; deliberate inoculation belongs in `biological_treatments`. Do not split a single composite label unless separate source-supported values exist. | Review synthesis of microbial findings is attributed to prior studies. | Abstract: microbial diversity and community structure after biochar treatments (WOS:001631731300001). | Chlorella application is a treatment, not a microbial measurement. |
| `measurements.greenhouse_gases` | Measured greenhouse-gas concentration, flux, or emission. | Explicit CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, or other greenhouse-gas measurements/emissions. | Carbon pool/stock without gas flux, general climate variables, or gas-related method names alone. | Gas emissions go here even when expressed as carbon-equivalent; do not duplicate under carbon without a distinct carbon-pool measurement. | Review-synthesized emissions are attributed to the literature reviewed. | Schematic: measured CH₄ flux. | Soil organic carbon stock is `carbon`, not a greenhouse-gas flux. |
| `measurements.plant_growth` | Measured plant growth, morphology, physiology, or development distinct from harvested production. | Height, biomass, root length/number, leaf area, photosynthetic traits, or growth stage metrics. | Harvested grain/biomass yield, crop presence, or treatment identity. | A plant trait is growth; harvested amount per area is yield. Report root morphology here, not under yield. | Review-reported growth outcomes are not measurements by the Review authors. | Abstract: root morphological characteristics assessed (WOS:001859510400001). | Rice grain yield is `yield`, not plant growth. |
| `measurements.yield` | Harvested crop or product output and its amount/rate. | Grain yield, crop yield, biomass yield at harvest, or production per area when explicit. | Yield components/plant morphology without harvested output, treatment, or projected regional production unless clearly reported as a study outcome. | Keep root traits in `plant_growth`; keep irrigation water productivity in `water_use` only when it is the measured water-use metric. | Review synthesis of yield gains is not a Review-author harvest measurement. | Abstract: rice yield reported for the pot experiment (WOS:001713822800001). | Root tip number is `plant_growth`, not yield. |
| `measurements.water_use` | Measured water amount, consumption, productivity, supply, demand, or water-use efficiency. | Water applied/consumed, evapotranspiration, irrigation volume, water productivity, or explicit water-use efficiency. | Irrigation method/regime, rainfall as background, or water quality alone. | Quantity/use is a measurement; how water is applied is `irrigation`; persistent wetness/cycle is `water_regime`. | Review-reported water-use metrics remain synthesis evidence. | Schematic: measured evapotranspiration or irrigation-water productivity. | `flooded versus shallow-wet` is a regime, not water use. |
| `measurements.other` | An explicitly measured variable not covered by the named measurement categories. | Named outcome/measurement that does not fit the above categories. | Treatment, method, scale, result claim without a measured variable, or an item that fits a narrower field. | Use only after checking every named category; preserve the measured entity rather than a whole analysis phrase. | Review-reported measurements are attributed to reviewed studies. | Abstract: phosphorus transport/removal where no phosphorus field exists (WOS:001713822800001). | Nitrogen transport is `nitrogen`, not `other`. |

## Methods and findings

| Field | Definition | Include | Exclude | Boundary rule | Review-paper rule | Example | Counterexample |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `methods` | Explicit named experimental method, model, measurement protocol, or analytical technique used in the study. | Direct method/model/technique names or a clearly described experimental method. | Target variable, treatment, result, generic statistical words such as “analysis” alone, or study scale alone. | Record the method phrase only; do not promote a scale label such as `field study` to a method. A named model is a method; its name alone does not determine `experimental_scale`. | Methods of reviewed papers remain attributed to those papers; do not represent them as Review-author experiments. | Abstract: PLUS–InVEST model and crop-coefficient method (WOS:001487641900001). | `field study` alone is an explicit scale phrase, not an analytical method. |
| `findings` | Explicit result of the present study or an attributed result synthesized by a Review. | Observed/estimated outcome, comparison, direction, magnitude, or relationship reported as a result. | Objective, background, method, recommendation, future implication, unsupported interpretation, or duplicate restatement. | A finding must answer what the study found. One-to-one output: do not extract the same result twice as a long sentence and short restatement. Preserve claim and evidence span exactly. | A Review may report synthesis results, but do not state them as experiments performed by the Review authors; preserve attribution where the abstract supplies it. | Abstract: field monitoring found seasonal water–salt transport patterns (WOS:001602265100001). | `To identify an optimized strategy...` is an objective, not a finding. |

## Cross-field decision order

1. Decide whether the phrase is a system description, applied intervention, measurement, method, or result before assigning a field.
2. For a treatment, ask whether the paper explicitly manipulated/applied it. Observed drivers and modeled scenarios are not treatment arms.
3. For salinity, require an explicit link to the actual system for an Article; for a Review label only the review scope and preserve that role.
4. For overlapping measurement concepts, prefer the specific analyte/outcome category above `other`.
5. Do not duplicate one value across fields to compensate for unclear ontology. If the contract does not resolve a boundary, leave it empty/unknown and flag for human review.
