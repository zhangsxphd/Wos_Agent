# v0.7 Contract v2 Ontology Decision Table

Draft rules only; requires human approval. Error counts are strict v1 historical holdout FP+FN from the frozen postmortem. The table is derived from Contract v1, the historical postmortem, human adjudications, and scientific field boundaries. It uses no v0.7 abstracts.

| Field | v1 issue | Historical error count | v2 proposed rule | Positive example | Negative example | Expected error class fixed | Regression risk |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| `soil_type` | Zero predictions across 18 Gold values; wording could be read as formal taxonomy only. | 18 (0 FP, 18 FN) | Soil/substrate descriptor, taxonomy optional, but require link to actual sample, experiment, study/site soil, or pot substrate. Exclude land use, region, target, and generic motivation. | Actual sampled saline-alkali soil → soil_type. | “Paddy field” or a regional saline-soil application target alone → no soil_type. | SOIL_TYPE_ABSTENTION; descriptor-vs-taxonomy | Over-extracting generic regional/application descriptors. |
| `salinity_context` | Background, application, Review scope, and actual system were conflated; many strict lexical mismatches. | 31 (16 FP, 15 FN); v2 residual 11 and 10 boundary matches | Article needs explicit actual-system or treatment-induced salinity relation. Prefer shortest span containing descriptor plus linkage. Review scope remains attributed. | “maize grown in severely saline-alkali soil” → actual-system context. | Generic motivation about saline soils → empty; pot system is not saline unless linked. | ACTUAL_SYSTEM_LINK_ERROR; TRUE_OVEREXTRACTION | Longer context spans can reduce lexical match; Review attribution may be lost. |
| `plant_growth` | Narrow operational reading missed plant physiology and biochemical response; 18 identical supports routed to `other`. | 32 (2 FP, 30 FN); 18 exact shared-span routes to `other` | Expand to plant phenotype/physiology, requiring explicit plant source/tissue. Includes photosynthesis, pigments, enzymes, ROS, proline, ions, and plant genes. | Leaf SOD/CAT, maize Na+, chlorophyll fluorescence → plant_growth. | Soil urease or microbial functional genes → not plant_growth. | PHYSIOLOGY_ROUTING_ERROR | Plant-source ambiguity can move soil or microbial measures into the field. |
| `measurements.other` | Residual category was not consistently last; 23 FP and 14 FN. | 37 (23 FP, 14 FN) | `OTHER_IS_LAST_RESORT=true`; inspect every named category in order, then use only for a measured variable without a named home. | Phosphorus measure or soil quality index → other. | Leaf proline / plant fluorescence → plant_growth; N analyte → nitrogen. | RESIDUAL_OTHER_ROUTING | Over-tight routing can leave real but uncategorized measures empty. |
| `microbial` | Community/function/network measures were incompletely routed; bulk soil enzymes risk being called microbial without attribution. | 25 (9 FP, 16 FN) | Include microbial abundance, diversity, taxa, genes, function, assembly, and networks. Require microbial attribution for enzymes. | `nosZ`, fungal diversity, community assembly, network connectivity → microbial. | Unattributed soil urease or plant antioxidant genes → not microbial. | MICROBIAL_ROUTING_ERROR; WRONG_CATEGORY | Gene/source ambiguity and enzyme attribution. |
| `carbon` | Explicit carbon fractions and properties could be missed or confused with materials/gases. | 16 (5 FP, 11 FN) | Include measured carbon pools, stocks, sequestration/mineralization, and explicit carbon fractions; gas flux remains greenhouse_gases. | Measured POC, MAOC, aromatic-C → carbon. | Biochar treatment or CH4 emission alone → not carbon. | TRUE_OMISSION; TOO_BROAD | Carbon-containing terms may be misread as measured carbon. |
| `soil_chemical` | General soil measures compete with specific analyte fields and `other`. | 15 (7 FP, 8 FN) | Include explicit pH, EC, CEC, exchangeable ions, soil salinity/ionic composition and nutrients; route N/carbon to specific fields and bulk enzyme/index to other. | Soil EC or CEC → soil_chemical. | Soil quality index / soil urease without microbial origin → other. | WRONG_CATEGORY; RESIDUAL_OTHER_ROUTING | Broad “soil chemical” reading can absorb N, C, or biological measures. |
| `fertilization` | Materials could be routed by name rather than function; 10 errors. | 10 (5 FP, 5 FN) | Fertilization means nutrient input or fertilizer management, not merely nutrient-containing material. | Applied NPK, urea rate, fertilizer substitution → fertilization. | Gypsum used for soil conditioning → not fertilization. | TREATMENT_VS_CONTROL; RESIDUAL_OTHER_ROUTING | Mixed products may have multiple roles if the text is unclear. |
| `amendments` | Conditioning/remediation role was not consistently separated from nutrient input. | 8 (4 FP, 4 FN) | Classify by explicit study role; gypsum/biochar/lime/pyroligneous vinegar as conditioning materials. Keep a separate matched NPK input in fertilization; no unsupported duplication. | Pyroligneous vinegar + separately specified matched NPK → amendment + fertilization, respectively. | Copying the whole vinegar treatment into fertilization → reject. | TREATMENT_VS_CONTROL | Dual-role materials could be under- or duplicated. |
| `experimental_scale` | Enum could not represent field-scale wording in a human-adjudicated case. | 0 v1 metric mismatches; 1 adjudicated representational limitation | Map field-scale variants to `field`. Named model is method only; select `model` only for overall modeling/simulation study design. | “field-scale experiment using HYDRUS” → field; HYDRUS in methods. | “HYDRUS model” alone → not scale=model. | METHOD_VS_SCALE | A physical scale may be omitted if only a named model is stated. |
| `author_interpretations` | Human Gold decisions exposed unclear separation from findings, objectives, and background. | Not scored as a field metric; multiple adjudications | Include explicit result-grounded association, causal interpretation, hypothesis, mechanistic interpretation, or management implication with anchor. Exclude background/objective/generic literature and finding duplicates. | “correlation analysis showed X related to Y” → association interpretation. | A result restated without distinct interpretation → no duplicate. | DUPLICATE_OR_BACKGROUND; CLAIM_TYPE_DISAGREEMENT | Overlap with findings may create duplicate claims. |
| `mechanisms_explicit` | Explicit mechanism summary was distinguished from a list of co-reported measurements by human adjudication. | Not scored as a field metric; 1 relevant accepted adjudication | Require explicit X affects Y through/by/via Z or equivalent. Prefer author summary; keep measured component variables in their measurement categories. | Explicit causal pathway sentence → mechanism. | A list of Na/K, ROS, and enzyme measurements without a stated relation → not a mechanism. | MECHANISM_OVERINFERENCE | Conservative extraction may miss compact but explicit mechanisms. |
