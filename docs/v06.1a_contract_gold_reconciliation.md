This is a contract audit of the v0.6.1 candidate, not a new benchmark. The frozen Evidence Field Contract v1 takes precedence over pre-contract adjudication where the two conflict. The audit loads only the seven existing iteration-1 development request payloads, not the shared corpus. The original Gold, strict v1 report, metric v2 report, iteration-1 responses, schema, validator, prompt, and holdout identities remain unchanged. No scores are recomputed.

## Experimental scale resolution

Only direct study-design evidence establishes scale. Explicit field, pot, greenhouse, laboratory/incubation, and modeling/simulation study descriptions map to their corresponding enum. A named computational model, regression model, or machine-learning model is a method by itself. If an explicit physical study scale coexists with a computational model, retain the physical scale and classify the computational model under methods. If two actual physical scales are explicit and neither is primary, use `unknown` with `multi_scale_ambiguity`.

## Review-derived evidence and downstream graph policy

Review-derived `study_system` values are **scope descriptors, not empirical system observations**. `document_types` on the canonical record identifies Review status; UID joins the record to its evidence sidecar, and the exact abstract support span remains the provenance anchor. No schema field is added.

For a Review record, downstream graph nodes and edges for treatments must be marked review-derived and must never assert that Review authors operated or applied the intervention. A graph may represent a summarized intervention only as an intervention reported in reviewed literature, linked to its source span and Review paper role. The current development Review has no treatment change-log entries or candidate treatment values, so this policy is prospective and no treatment values are rewritten.

The scoped reconciliation ledger classifies 21 entries `CONTRACT_CONSISTENT`, 13 `CONTRACT_CONFLICT`, and 6 `NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW`; it does not re-adjudicate all 87 field/finding rows.
