## Effects of mild alternate wetting and drying irrigation and rice straw application on N2O emissions in rice cultivation
[10.5194/soil-8-645-2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/soil-8-645-2022)；比较 `10.5194/soil-8-645-2022#result1:0`；Table 1 C11 versus C6; identical fertilizer and isotope treatment; n=3 pots；PDF 物理页 [2, 3, 7]。
研究对象与设计：rice paddy Alfisol; salinity not specified；randomized block rice pot experiment, 2020。
处理：AWD 15U（intermittent irrigation at -15 kPa; 225 kg N/ha; no straw; 15N-labelled urea）；对照：CF 15U（continuous flooding 3–5 cm; 225 kg N/ha; no straw; 15N-labelled urea）。
原始值：4.48 ± 0.12 vs 4.02 ± 0.3 kg N2O/ha；误差类型 SE/SE；显著性：Table 1 letters b vs c; Duncan p<0.05, original table。
主题纳入：符合主题的候选，待来源核查；实验单位：pot，每处理 3；设计有效性：记录了独立重复，仍需核查随机化和实施细节。每处理3个独立盆
标准化值：4.48 ± 0.12 vs 4.02 ± 0.3 kg N2O/ha；处理相对对照变化 11.443%（程序计算）。换算依据：original already N2O mass；研究内变化不代表统计显著性或因果效应。
未解决事项：Source selection and design manually checked; independent treatment replication and cross-study context must be verified.。

## Alternate Wetting and Drying of Rice Reduced CH4 Emissions but Triggered N2O Peaks in a Clayey Soil of Central Italy
[10.1016/S1002-0160(15)60063-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1002-0160(15)60063-7)；比较 `10.1016/S1002-0160(15)60063-7#result1:0`；PDF page 8 results text, 2012 AWD vs PF; paper pages 2–3 describe fields and pseudo-replicates；PDF 物理页 [2, 3, 8]。
研究对象与设计：rice paddy clay soil in central Italy；2012 and 2013 field experiment, one field per water regime。
处理：AWD（intermittent drying to -15 cm; Entec 46 with nitrification inhibitor, 120 kg N/ha; one field）；对照：PF（permanent flooding; Entec 46 with nitrification inhibitor, 120 kg N/ha; one field）。
原始值：4.91 ± 未报告 vs 0.8 ± 未报告 kg N-N2O/ha；误差类型 unknown/unknown；显著性：unknown: separate cumulative comparison significance not present in selected passage。
主题纳入：符合主题的候选，待来源核查；实验单位：field，每处理 1；设计有效性：处理层重复不足或未知（伪重复）。One field per water regime; 3 chambers in 2012 and 4 in 2013 are pseudo-replicates; independent water-treatment replication not established
标准化值：7.716 ± 未报告 vs 1.257 ± 未报告 kg N2O/ha；处理相对对照变化 513.75%（程序计算）。换算依据：N2O-N to N2O: molecular mass ratio 44/28；研究内变化不代表统计显著性或因果效应。
未解决事项：候选表注对比较范围措辞需核对；原文讨论另报2012增加307%；由正文4.91与0.80计算为513.75%，两处不一致，未解决。。
